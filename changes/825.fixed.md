A paged diff finds its renames once and keeps them for its later
pages, in a cache of at most 512 MiB whose entries live one call
deadline and whose bytes the scion_diff_rename_cache_bytes gauge
shows; before, every page walked the whole diff again and reran the
rename search. A graph query stops after 1,000,000 visits of its walk
with RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED, logged with the count, where only the call's
deadline stopped it (#825).
