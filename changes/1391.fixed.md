Shared-limit waits end at a 30 s busy bound (#1391): every wait for the
blame, diff, graph-query, read-budget or derive-walk limit that finds no
room within it fails at once with a retryable `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` whose
message says `busy: no <limit> free; retry later`, instead of waiting
until the call's own deadline. The wait's count lands in a new metric
series, `scion_busy_timeouts_total{limit}`, with one label per limit:
`blame`, `diff`, `graph_query`, `read_budget`, `derive_walk`.
