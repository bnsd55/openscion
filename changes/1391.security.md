Under `--access`, the small server queues get a per-principal share
(#1391): one principal holds at most half, rounded up, of the blame
slots (2 of 4), the diff slots (2 of 4), the graph-query slots (2 of 4),
the derive walks (4 of 8), the read budget (128 of 256 MiB) and the
upload permits (8 of 16). A call that would take its principal over the
share is refused at once, before any wait, with a `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED`
that names the share - it is the principal's own cap, not a busy
server. A principal with no rule, or with folder read rules only, keeps
its old answer. With the single-principal mode (`SCION_TOKEN` or
`--token-file`), every caller is the same principal, so the shares are
off and the whole limits stand. The caps bound bytes in flight, not
bytes at rest: the disk stays the operator's to watch
(`scion_data_free_bytes`, the `DiskNearlyFull` and `DiskFull` alerts,
and the "Disk full" runbook).
